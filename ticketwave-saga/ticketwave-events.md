# Kata: TicketWave Events

## Description
An event ticketing company wants to sell tickets online for concerts, sports events, conferences, and local venues. Customers can search events, buy tickets, receive digital passes, and request refunds.

## Users
Small daily traffic, but massive spikes when popular events go on sale.

## Requirements
- Allow users to search events by city, artist, venue, and date.
- Allow users to purchase tickets.
- Allow users to select seats where applicable.
- Accept online payment.
- Issue digital tickets.
- Support refunds and cancellations.
- Notify users about purchases, event changes, and cancellations.
- Support national promotions.
- Support venue-specific promotions.
- Prevent duplicate ticket sales.
- Detect suspicious purchasing behavior.
- Support mobile tickets.
- Integrate with payment providers and venue access systems.

## Additional Context
- Demand can spike dramatically for popular events.
- Some venues control their own seating maps.
- Some tickets are general admission; others are assigned seats.
- Fraud and bots are a concern.
- Events may be postponed, canceled, or moved.
- Future plans include resale marketplace features.
